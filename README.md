Premier League Exploratory Data Analysis (2018–19)

Project Overview

This project explores the 2018–19 English Premier League season using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib. The goal is to identify patterns in match outcomes, team scoring performance, home advantage, and the relationship between shots on target and goals.

The project demonstrates practical data analysis skills, including data inspection, aggregation, feature creation, statistical correlation, and visualization.

Research Questions

How frequently do home teams win, draw, or lose?

Which teams score the most goals?

How do teams' home and away win rates compare?

Is there a relationship between shots on target and goals scored?

Which teams combine strong offensive and defensive performance?

Tools and Technologies

Python

Pandas

Matplotlib

Jupyter Notebook

Key Findings

Home advantage: Home teams won 47.63% of matches, compared with 33.68% for away teams. Draws accounted for 18.68%.

Goal scoring: Manchester City led the analysis with 95 total goals, followed by Liverpool with 89 and Arsenal with 73.

Home vs. away performance: Arsenal had a 73.68% home win rate and a 36.84% away win rate, a difference of 36.84 percentage points.

Shots and goals: Shots on target and goals scored had a Pearson correlation coefficient of 0.580, indicating a moderate positive linear association.

Offensive and defensive performance: Manchester City and Liverpool combined high average scoring with low average goals conceded per match.

These findings describe the 2018–19 season and should not be interpreted as predictions of future performance.
